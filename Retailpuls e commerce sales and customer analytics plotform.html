Task 6 (20 Marks)

Power BI Star-Schema & DAX Measures

Import retailpulse_ecommerce_dataset.csv into Power BI Desktop and paste these DAX measures into your model:

Total Gross Sales = SUM(Fact_Orders[Gross_Sales_USD])
Total Net Revenue = SUM(Fact_Orders[Net_Revenue_USD])
Total Net Profit  = SUM(Fact_Orders[Net_Profit_USD])
Total Orders      = DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Orders[Order_ID])
Active Customers  = DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Orders[Customer_ID])

Net Profit Margin % =
    DIVIDE([Total Net Profit], [Total Net Revenue], 0)

Avg Order Value (AOV) =
    DIVIDE([Total Net Revenue], [Total Orders], 0)

Effective Discount % =
    DIVIDE([Total Gross Sales] - [Total Net Revenue], [Total Gross Sales], 0)

Returned Orders =
    CALCULATE([Total Orders], Fact_Orders[Returned] = "Yes")

Return Rate % =
    DIVIDE([Returned Orders], [Total Orders], 0)

Avg Delivery SLA = AVERAGE(Fact_Orders[Delivery_Days])
Avg CSAT Rating  = AVERAGE(Fact_Orders[Customer_Rating])
